About Us

The spirit of Indian street food

Angadi Food Truck celebrates the vibrant energy of chai stalls, roadside carts, and late-night food markets. Our menu blends authentic masalas, crisp textures, and bold flavors into a premium street food experience that travels to your neighborhood.

Every dish is designed to feel familiar, yet refined — the kind of food that draws a crowd, sparks cravings, and becomes the highlight of any event.
